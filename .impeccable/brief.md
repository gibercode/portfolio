## Direction contract

THESIS: A near-empty page where a Soto/Cruz-Diez vibrating line field and a kinetic name react to the visitor; refuses the dark bento grid with a tech-stack icon wall.
OWN-WORLD: Cool white ground #f1f3f4, ink #0c0c0d, one chromatic accent at a time (cyan #00b8d9 / magenta #ea1e7d / yellow #ffd400) spent only on the line-field color strips, hover motifs and the primary button. Variable grotesk with a width axis (Anybody) for name and titles, Hanken Grotesk for text. 1px vertical lines are the only ornament.
STORY: Visitor sees a name alive under their cursor, understands a full-stack developer who ships whole products, believes it via real repos, and writes to him.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed canvas of 1px vertical lines bending around the pointer with a cyan-magenta-yellow strip zone behind; GIBERSON / LARA in giant variable type bottom-left, letters widen near the cursor; role line and a filled ink Contact button bottom-right; top bar holds ES/EN and three palette swatches.
FORM: Kinetic op-art field (rolled direction 'Cinetismo venezolano', seed 20b3f14f, reroll 1), softened toward sober via ma-style emptiness.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
